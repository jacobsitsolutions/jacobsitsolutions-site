    Confirm the domain, read permissions, required module, output location and the review's purpose before collection.

    Edit a copy of the worksheet. Record an owner, reviewer, decision, reason, date and ticket reference.

    Preserve the original output and retain approvals and evidence of any separately authorized changes.

Know the scope

Is this the review you need?

A potential fit

  • You administer one on-premises AD domain.
  • You regularly prepare user-account and group reviews.
  • You want local reports and a worksheet for human review.
  • You can use an approved PowerShell tool with documented prerequisites.

Outside this pilot

  • Entra ID, Microsoft 365 and other applications.
  • All effective permissions or administrative access paths.
  • Automated approval routing or access removal.
  • Computer accounts, managed service accounts and multi-domain consolidation.
Real AD testing is still ahead.

Live collection is intended for Windows PowerShell 5.1 with Microsoft's ActiveDirectory module. Offline checks pass on Windows PowerShell 5.1 and PowerShell 7 using synthetic objects and mocked directory queries. These results do not establish live-domain compatibility or production readiness. The lab is being prepared; no real AD test results are available yet.

Before release

A few practical answers.

Can I buy or download the toolkit today?

No. AD Access Review is an internal pilot. You can inspect the synthetic sample and use the free review-preparation guide and blank templates. A toolkit download, price and purchase link will follow validation and release preparation.

Does an inactivity flag mean an account should be disabled?

No. Replicated logon timestamps can be delayed or missing. Service accounts and emergency accounts need context. The indicators prompt a human review; they do not authorize removal.

Will the pilot upload my directory information?

The pilot writes local report files and implements no vendor-upload or telemetry workflow. Protect those files under your organization's access and retention rules.

Does a completed collection prove a successful audit?

No. Collection status describes the requested queries and modeled relationships within the stated scope. It does not establish security, compliance or completion of the human review. File hashes also do not authenticate an unsigned manifest.

Do I need to schedule a call?

No call is required to inspect the sample or ask a product question. Our direction is documented, self-service software with product questions by email.
