Common questions

Before you get started.

Can I download or buy a tool today?

No. The tool areas shown here are planned. This page will link to released tools when downloads, documentation, and any applicable pricing are available.

Do I need to schedule a call?

Our planned products are intended for self-service use. You can explore the roadmap here and send product questions or feature suggestions by email.

Do you provide managed IT or emergency support?

Jacobs IT Solutions is focused on software and automation. This site does not offer managed IT contracts, phone support, or emergency troubleshooting services.

Will the tools change my environment?

Permissions, data handling, and any changes a tool can make will be documented for each release. Review those details before running a tool.

Have a product question or feature idea?

Email tim@jacobsitsolutions.com. Please keep messages free of credentials and sensitive environment data.
